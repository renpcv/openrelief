3D model to bas-relief tool

Models

    Drag to orbit, right-drag to pan, scroll to zoom. Click a model to select it, Delete removes it. With the Scale tool, drag an axis to stretch or the centre cube to scale evenly. The relief is taken from the current view.

    Export

    Meshes are watertight solids in millimetres. PNG is normalised 0–1; EXR and PFM hold heights in mm. Relief depth runs from the lowest point to the highest; hollows can sit below the background.

    The relief method follows published research on gradient-domain bas-relief.

    T. Weyrich, J. Deng, C. Barnes, S. Rusinkiewicz, A. Finkelstein. Digital Bas-Relief from 3D Scenes. ACM Transactions on Graphics 26(3) (Proc. SIGGRAPH), 2007. The main source: working on depth gradients, turning silhouettes and occlusion edges into controlled steps, and compressing slopes with a log curve. Project page, PDF

    J. Kerber, A. Tevs, A. Belyaev, R. Zayer, H.-P. Seidel. Feature Sensitive Bas Relief Generation. IEEE International Conference on Shape Modeling and Applications (SMI), 2009, pp. 148–154. Informed the detail-emphasis step. DOI, PDF

    R. Fattal, D. Lischinski, M. Werman. Gradient Domain High Dynamic Range Compression. ACM Transactions on Graphics 21(3) (Proc. SIGGRAPH), 2002, pp. 249–256. The origin of the “compress the gradients, then solve a Poisson equation” idea that the bas-relief papers build on. Project page

    Also built on

    Solver: conjugate gradients preconditioned by aggregation multigrid; standard numerical methods rather than a specific paper. General background: W. Briggs, V. E. Henson, S. McCormick, A Multigrid Tutorial, 2nd ed., SIAM, 2000.

    Smoothing: a Gaussian blur approximated by three box-filter passes, a common image-processing technique.

    3D view and file loading: three.js (MIT licence), including its OBJ and STL loaders and its orbit and transform controls.

    Export formats: written from their published specifications: PNG, OpenEXR, PFM, and the widely documented binary STL and Wavefront OBJ formats.
